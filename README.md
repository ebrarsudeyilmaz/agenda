# Agenda

## What is Agenda?
It's an undated planner, so you can use it any year. You can take notes, color in the books you've read, etc.

## What is the story behind it?
It is my first Flutter project. I wrote it without AI; AI only reviewed my code.

## What are the features?
There are many pages for planning. You can also take notes about your feelings, the books you've read and the movies you've watched. If you want to see what you did in a year, these pages are for you. You can write on your tablet or type on your phone (it doesn't matter which phone you use).

Here are my goals:
- [ ] Daily, weekly and monthly pages
- [ ] Mood tracker
- [ ] Books read, movies watched
- [ ] Research notes
- [ ] Birthdays
- [ ] Handwriting with a stylus
- [ ] Palm rejection

## What will I do for privacy?
The app doesn't connect to the internet. Your data will be encrypted, and it stays on your device. You don't have to log in with an account.
